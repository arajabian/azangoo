Horizon visibility at sunset
Horizon visibility is how many kilometres you can see clearly through the air at that moment. The number comes from the weather forecast for this place at sunset. The new crescent is thin and sits low near the horizon, so fog, dust or smoke can hide it. 15 km or more: good; 6 to 14 km: medium; under 6 km: poor. This is only a weather forecast; it does not change the crescent ruling.

About Azangoo

We launched Azangoo in 2011, back when it ran on Google Maps, and for a long time it worked well and without any trouble. After a while, however, Google stopped providing us with its map service, and offering prayer times on Azangoo became difficult.

This year, in 2026, by God’s grace we were given the chance once again to serve you, dear worshippers, this time with a fresh design, modern features, and a page that works comfortably on your phone.

Our aim is to bring the sound of the adhan to your home, your workplace and wherever else you may be, no matter where in the world you live. That is why we calculate the prayer times for the place you choose on the map, and alongside them we offer the qibla direction, the sound of the adhan, the daily and Ramadan supplications, the crescent moon sighting and a calendar, in Persian, English, Urdu and Arabic.

Azangoo is not affiliated with any organization, institution, party or group, governmental or non-governmental, anywhere in the world.

We hope we have been able to render some service, however small, to those who pray. Without the grace and mercy of God Almighty and the care of the Master of the Age, Imam al-Mahdi (may God hasten his relief), this work would not have been possible for us. Finally, we humbly ask for your prayers: please do not deprive us of them at the time of adhan and prayer, and never forget to pray for the hastening of the relief of our master, the Imam of the Age.
