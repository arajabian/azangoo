Accuracy of times and rulings

Prayer times and other times are calculated with astronomical and mathematical methods and may differ slightly from official announcements or local observation.

The Hijri date and moon sighting shown on the site are the site’s own calculation and an estimate; they are not an official announcement and may differ from the announcements of official bodies.

Crescent visibility is estimated with the Odeh V-criterion, an empirical rule that only estimates whether the new crescent can be seen; it is not an official announcement. A crescent calculated as visible but close to the threshold may need an optical aid to be seen.

Source of the criterion: Mohammad Sh. Odeh, “New Criterion for Lunar Crescent Visibility”, Experimental Astronomy, vol. 18 (2004), pp. 39–64. Search for the article on Google Scholar

Please check religious questions with a qualified scholar or the official body of your area. This site does not issue rulings.
